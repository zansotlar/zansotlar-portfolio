AI Banner Generation Tool

An AI tool I built at PM, poslovni mediji to make banner production faster and more consistent.

Type
Professional work · PM, poslovni mediji
Platform

Tools & platforms

The challenge

A media and marketing team produces banners constantly: for campaigns, articles and social channels, in many formats. Doing each one by hand slows down the whole editorial and publishing rhythm.

My role

I built the tool as part of my role as AI & Marketing Automation Specialist, and fitted it into the team's day-to-day production work.

Approach

  1. 01Looked at how banners were actually being produced and where time was lost.
  2. 02Built an AI-powered tool that generates banner layouts from a short input.
  3. 03Connected it with the wider workflow: editorial planning, social media preparation and CMS tasks.

Key functionality

  • Generates banner designs from a brief
  • Keeps output consistent across formats
  • Built for everyday use by a working team

Value

A practical example of AI applied inside a real agency workflow: less repetitive production work, more time for the ideas.

01AI agent · Customer assistance

AI Credit Advisory Agent

A conversational assistant built around credit advisory: first answers in plain language, at any hour.

Type
Personal project
Platform
Agent X
Tools & platforms
Agent X · Conversation design · Prompt design
Open the live agent↗

The challenge

People researching a loan usually start with the same handful of questions, often outside office hours, and often before they are ready to speak to an advisor. Answering them one by one is slow for the provider and frustrating for the customer.

My role

Independent project: I defined the use case, designed the conversation and built and configured the agent on Agent X.

Approach

  1. 01Scoped the agent to one job: credit advisory and customer assistance.
  2. 02Wrote its instructions, tone of voice and boundaries so answers stay clear and on topic.
  3. 03Published it as a shareable chat that anyone can open in the browser.

Key functionality

  • Answers common credit questions in plain language
  • Guides the user toward the right next step
  • Available around the clock through a public link

Value

A working example of how a financial provider could take repetitive first-line questions off its team and give customers faster answers.

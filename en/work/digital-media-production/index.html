06Broadcast · Editorial · Social

Digital Media & Content Production

National sports broadcasting, live entertainment TV, student journalism and social media marketing.

Type
Professional work
Platform
RTV Slovenija · POP TV · Student.si · Media Element
Tools & platforms
Video editing · Live production · Meta Business Suite · Canva · CapCut · Writing

The challenge

Media work is about two things: getting it right and getting it out on time. I have done both on very different stages, from the sports desk to the studio floor to a client's Instagram.

My role

Sports editor at RTV Slovenija, event organiser and production support at POP TV, article writer at Student.si, and digital marketing specialist at Media Element.

Approach

  1. 01RTV Slovenija: preparing, logging and editing sports footage and archive highlights under strict deadlines.
  2. 02POP TV: logistics, coordination and on-location support for live entertainment shows.
  3. 03Student.si: writing for a student audience and sharpening headlines; recent articles reached roughly 400–1,000 views each.
  4. 04Media Element: social media management, ad creatives and content planning for clients.

Key functionality

  • Broadcast editing under deadline
  • Live TV production support
  • Audience-focused writing
  • Social media and ad creatives

Value

The production discipline behind everything else I do: deadlines, teamwork and content made for a real audience.

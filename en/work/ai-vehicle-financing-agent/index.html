AI Vehicle Financing Sales Agent

A Voiceflow assistant that walks car buyers through financing questions and supports the sales conversation.

Type
Personal project
Platform
Voiceflow
Tools & platforms
Voiceflow · Conversation design · Flow logic
Open the Voiceflow project↗

The challenge

Buying a car on finance raises many questions at once: options, steps, what to prepare. Sales teams answer them again and again, and a buyer who doesn't get answers quickly tends to drop off.

My role

Independent project: I designed the conversation flow and built the assistant in Voiceflow.

Approach

  1. 01Mapped the typical financing journey of a car buyer.
  2. 02Turned it into a structured flow that combines guided steps with open questions.
  3. 03Designed hand-off points where a human salesperson takes over.

Key functionality

  • Guides customers through vehicle financing questions
  • Structured flow with clear next steps
  • Built to support, not replace, the sales team

Value

Shows how conversational AI can keep a buyer moving through a financing journey and hand warmer leads to sales.
